The real FM-1 firmware, compiled for the browser: the same code, the same 240 × 240 screen and the same controls as on the synth. The walk, redrawn for the FM-1’s screen (2.8 cm a side).

Buttons

Keys · 27

Walk

Arrow keys (EDIT up, PLAY down, SEQ left, REC right on the FM-1). Space is LFO, the action. R is SCL, the chord grid. L is HOME.

Play

Near a place the synth wakes up: the keys play its chords (computer row A to K). At home, switch the synth on with LFO, then each key holds its chord.

Knobs

ALGO the sky (cycle, day, night, storm), PRESET the place's sound, K1 to K4 wind, drone, steps and synth. Your walk is kept when you go home.
