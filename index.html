Banquise.

An OSR-1 prototype: you set out from your igloo across a wide ice field. Wind, a drone, your muffled steps. Find the places, step inside, go fishing, recover the captain’s journal.

The quest

Obsimo lives in an igloo. The notebook on his desk lists what’s left to do: the captain’s journal pages, the lighthouse to relight, the ice flute, the lost drum machine. Then the old boat waits for someone to ring its bell. Ten outfits are hidden around.

The places

The igloo, the big hole, the boat, the cave, the forest, the fire, the lighthouse, the ice rink, the seals (they sing, you repeat with keys 1 to 4). Yucca passes by on her sled, penguins migrate and echo what you play, bottles wash up on the shore (some hide a treasure map). At night an old fisherman sits by the big hole. Nine tapes are won through challenges (the trophy board gives hints), seven decorations go in the trophy room, through the arch at the back of the igloo.

Controls

ALGORITHM: day/night cycle, or a fixed sky. PRESET: the sound. SCL (R): the chord grid. ENV (I): instruments you found. HOME (L): back home. LFO (Space) = Action. Snow + 3 × LFO = a rhythmic snowman (it melts after a minute). Your game saves in this browser (“New game” at the top starts over).

Start the adventure over?

Are you really sure? There’s no going back: you’ll lose your outfits, your tapes, your fish, your decorations, your journal pages and all your trophies. Obsimo will wake up alone in his igloo, like on the very first day.
